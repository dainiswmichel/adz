Platform Compliance

adz-world is a WordPress plugin distributed through WordPress.org and the website adz-world.com. The following acknowledgments apply to both.

WordPress.org

The plugin is free software under the GNU General Public License version 3. It is written to the WordPress Coding Standards, enqueues all of its scripts and styles, and contacts no outside service unless a site administrator configures it. Every outside service it can contact is disclosed in its readme, with what is sent, when, and links to that service's terms and privacy policy.

Advertising Networks

A site owner may place display advertising from networks including Google AdSense, Google Ad Manager, Taboola, Outbrain and Teads, Media.net, Amazon Publisher Services, Ezoic, Mediavine, Raptive, Monumetric, MGID, RevContent, Carbon, Criteo, Dianomi, Snigel and Freestar. Their code loads only in the slot where the site owner placed it, and they operate under their own terms and policies.

Affiliate Programs and Product Services

Sponsored links and product results can come from Amazon Associates and the Amazon Creators API, eBay Partner Network and the eBay Browse API, Etsy, Best Buy, Walmart and Impact, Awin, CJ Affiliate, Rakuten Advertising, ClickBank, Apple Services Performance Partners, AvantLink and Travelpayouts. Each is used only with the site owner's own account, and each pays the site owner under its own agreement.

Search Engines

Public adz-world.com pages may be indexed by search engines such as Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo. Reader sessions, preferences and journeys are never public and are not indexed.

Privacy Law

adz-world is built so that a visitor who only looks is not recorded, and so that a reader's information is kept on a profile only with that reader's own consent. It supports WordPress's personal-data export and erase tools.
