User Data Deletion

We respect your right to privacy and provide options for managing your data.

If You Are a Reader

If you never used the invitation panel, nothing exists to delete. If you did, what you typed is held in your own browser. Use the remove control beside any entry, or clear site data for that website. Deletion is immediate and complete, and there is no copy anywhere else.

If Your Site Is Connected

To request deletion of your adz.world account and associated data, contact us through the platform. Confirm your identity using the administrator address the site was registered with. We will process your request within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your publisher profile and access tokens
  • Your site URL, title and registered administrator email
  • Adz you published through the network
  • Your referral code and its associations

What May Be Retained

  • Transaction records required for accounting and tax compliance
  • Anonymised usage statistics with no personal identifiers
  • Legal hold data where required by law

Third Parties

Advertising and affiliate networks a site connects receive data directly from your browser under their own terms. Deleting what adz.world holds does not delete what they hold. See our Privacy Policy for each provider.

Reactivation

After deletion you may register again with the same or a different site. Deleted data cannot be recovered.
