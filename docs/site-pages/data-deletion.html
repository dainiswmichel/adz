User Data Deletion

We respect your right to privacy. Most of what adz-world keeps lives in your own browser session, so you can delete it yourself at any time.

How to Delete Your Data

  • Close your browser: adz-world's cookies are session cookies and end when the browser closes. Clearing this site's cookies deletes your session immediately.
  • Delete a refinement: open it in the adz hub and choose Delete. Change a rating by moving its slider.
  • If you saved adz requests with your profile: untick Save my adz requests with my profile on your profile page. Everything kept on your profile is deleted.
  • Journeys attached to your profile: ask the owner of that site to use WordPress's built-in Erase Personal Data tool. It deletes your adz journeys and saved adz requests. The Export Personal Data tool gives you a copy first if you want one.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your categories and refinements
  • Your ratings
  • Your adz journeys attached to your profile
  • Your choice to save adz requests with your profile

What Is Deleted Automatically

  • Session data, when your browser session ends, and preferences after 24 hours
  • Journeys not attached to a profile, after the number of days the site owner sets (30 days by default)
  • Everything adz-world stored, if the site owner deletes the plugin

What May Be Retained

Anonymous journeys remain until their retention period ends, because they are not linked to you and cannot be found by your name or email address. Information collected by a third-party advertising network or affiliate network is held by that network under its own policy; ask that network directly.
