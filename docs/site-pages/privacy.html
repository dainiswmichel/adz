Our Commitment to Privacy

adz-world serves advertising by permission. We do not watch what you do to guess what you want. You tell us what you are looking for, and the adz follow what you said. This page explains exactly what is kept, where, when, and for how long.

If You Only Look

A visitor who only looks at a page with adz is not recorded. No session is started, no adz-world cookie is set, and nothing about the visit is written down. Your IP address is not read or sent anywhere by adz-world.

When You Tell Us What You Want

Typing a category, adding a refinement, or rating an adz starts a browser session. The session is named by the time of that first action plus five random characters, for example 2026y-11m-17d-UTC13h-37m-12s-a7k2q. It holds only the categories, refinements and ratings you entered. It does not need your name, email address or postal address, and it is not joined to other information about you.

Three session cookies make this work: one naming your session, one holding your ratings, and the standard PHP session cookie. Session cookies end when you close your browser. Your preferences also start fresh after 24 hours.

If You Have a Profile

If you are signed in to a site that uses adz-world, your adz requests are kept with your profile only if two things are both true: the site owner allows it, and you choose Save my adz requests with my profile on your profile page. Without that choice, a signed-in reader is treated like any visitor: browser session only. Turning the choice off deletes the categories, refinements and ratings kept on your profile.

Adz Journeys (AdzLogs)

After your first action, the site keeps a journey of what you did with adz, so the site owner can see which adz people respond to. A journey records the rotation and slot, the exact adz you acted on, your rating, and the words you entered, in order. Adz you only look at are never recorded. A journey contains no IP address, name or email address. It is attached to your account only if you chose to save adz requests with your profile.

Journeys are kept on the site's own server. They are deleted after the number of days the site owner sets (30 days by default), and are removed entirely if the plugin is deleted.

How We Use Your Information

  • To select adz that match the categories and refinements you entered.
  • To stop showing an adz you rated poorly, and to remember the adz you liked.
  • To show the site owner which adz readers respond to (AdzLogs).

We do not build a behavioral profile, sell your information, or share it with advertisers.

Advertising and Product Services

Every connection to an outside service is off until a site administrator turns it on. When a site connects a product provider (such as Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Best Buy, Apple, AvantLink or Travelpayouts), the site's server sends that provider only the category and refinements as search words. It never sends your IP address, session identifier or account. When you follow a sponsored link, your browser goes to that network, which receives the normal information any website receives and pays the site owner under its own terms.

A site owner may also place a third-party advertising network in a slot, such as Google AdSense, Google Ad Manager, Taboola, Outbrain, Criteo or Amazon Advertising. Those networks run under their own terms and may collect information on their own. adz-world cannot control that collection, but the information mark on each adz panel tells you when it can be present. The full list of services, what each receives, and links to their terms and privacy policies is in the plugin's readme on WordPress.org.

Data Storage and Security

Everything adz-world keeps stays on the server of the site you are visiting. Session data is held by that server's PHP session handler. Profile data and journeys are stored in that site's WordPress database. Credentials a site owner saves for outside services are never shown on public pages.

Your Rights

You can see, change and delete your adz requests at any time. See Data Deletion for how.

Changes

When what adz-world collects changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated.
