Our Commitment to Privacy

adz.world is permission-based advertising. This Privacy Policy explains what the adz.world website and WordPress plugin collect, and what they do not.

What We Collect

By default the plugin collects nothing and connects to no external service. A site serves only the adz created on it, and no reader data leaves that site's server.

If a reader uses the invitation panel to say what they are shopping for, that text is held in their own browser. It is not an account, it is not sent to us, and it is not readable by the site owner.

If a Site Connects to the Network

A site administrator may switch on a connection to the adz.world network. With it on, we receive:

  • The site's publisher ID, access tokens and referral code
  • A reader's IP address when an adz is shown, so the same adz is not repeated
  • An adz title, content and categories when it is published or deleted
  • The site URL, title and administrator email at registration

We do not sell this and we do not build advertising profiles of readers from it.

Third-Party Services

A site owner may connect advertising networks and affiliate programmes using their own accounts. Those providers receive data directly from a reader's browser under their own terms. Review each provider's privacy policy. Nothing is sent to any provider until a site administrator enables it.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update or request deletion of your personal data. See our Data Deletion page.
