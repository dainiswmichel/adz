What adz-world Is

adz-world is a WordPress plugin and the website adz-world.com. The plugin lets a site owner run rotations of adz that readers can shape by saying what they are looking for. It is free software, licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3, and distributed through WordPress.org.

Readers

Using adz-world as a reader is free and needs no account. You choose whether to tell a site what you are looking for. Any adz you see because of a third-party network placed by the site owner is governed by that network's terms. Purchases you make through a sponsored link are between you and the merchant.

Site Owners

A site owner who installs the plugin is responsible for their own site, for the adz they publish, and for the accounts they connect: advertising networks, affiliate programs and product services. Each of those is governed by its own agreement with the site owner. adz-world adds the site owner's own identifiers to sponsored links so that the network credits the site owner. adz-world does not receive or redistribute that commission.

A site owner is responsible for telling their readers what their site collects, including through any third-party network they place, and for their own privacy policy. The plugin suggests privacy-policy text for the site owner to use.

The adz-world.com Connection

The plugin's optional connection to adz-world.com is off unless a site owner turns it on. When on, it is used to exchange adz between participating sites and, if requested, read-only earnings statements. Switching it off stops all exchange.

Acceptable Use

Adz must be lawful, honest and not misleading. Do not use adz-world to distribute malware, to deceive readers, or to collect readers' information without their consent.
